Customer care / Returns & exchanges

Make it right.

We want you to live in your KESHEV pieces. If something isn’t right, request an exchange within 7 days of delivery.

  1. Write to hello@keshev.com with your order number.
  2. Keep the piece unworn, unwashed and with its tags attached.
  3. We’ll arrange the next step and share the exchange timeline.

Sale items and personalised items are final sale. Refund and exchange eligibility is subject to inspection.
